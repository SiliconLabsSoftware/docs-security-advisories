## Description

- In high traffic environments, the RCP may send a SPI packet with a corrupt header. Upon receiving the corrupt packet, the host will reset the RCP device leading to a temporary denial of service 
- The error condition is immediately cleared once the device has been reset 

### Fix/Workaround

- Users affected by [CVE-2025-2329](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-2329) should upgrade their OpenThread SDK according to the table below.
