## CVE ID(s)

- [CVE-2025-12986](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-12986) is reserved for this issue.

## Description

- When a WF200 or WGM160P device is configured to operate as an Access Point, it may be vulnerable to a denial-of service triggered by a malformed packet.
    - The device may recover automatically or require a hard reset. The host can be used to reset the WF200 device.

### Fix/Workaround

- No fix is available for this issue.
