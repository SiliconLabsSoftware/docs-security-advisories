## CVE ID(s)

- [CVE-2025-12131](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-12131) is reserved for this issue.

## Description

- A truncated 802.15.4 packet may cause an assert in the Zigbee or Multiprotocol stack leading to a temporary denial of service.
- The device resets immediately, clearing the error.

### Fix/Workaround

- Impacted SiSDK users should upgrade to Ember ZNet 9.0.0 or later delivered as part of SiSDK 2025.12.0, or later.

- Impacted GSDK users should upgrade to Ember ZNet v7.5.2 or later delivered as part of GSDK 4.5.1 or later.

- Instructions for downloading/updating the SDK and for upgrading a project can be found in the Simplicity Studio Users Guide
