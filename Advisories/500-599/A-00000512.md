## Description

- In a multi-protocol environment, such as OpenThread border router (OTBR) combined with Z3 Gateway, the OpenThread process may be forced to wait to send data until the Zigbee process has finished sending data. During this time, data for the OpenThread process is buffered. When the channel becomes free, the Thread data is unpacked from the buffer but the location used to unpack the data is incorrect.

- One possible way for this situation to occur is to force a significant amount of Zigbee traffic. This type of heavy traffic could be maliciously caused by a large number of Zigbee end devices to join the network in a short period of time. This could result in a denial of service (DOS) attack.

- The observed impact is for the OTBR process running on the host platform to terminate unexpectedly due to improper error handling of corrupt data.
