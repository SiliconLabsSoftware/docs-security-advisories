## Description

- Series 2 devices with differential power analysis (DPA) countermeasures are not reseeded. This means that the countermeasures are not sufficiently random and will eventually repeat.

- Countermeasures for DPA within SYMCRYPTO engine on SixG301xxx devices are also not sufficiently random and will eventually repeat.

    - KSU keys using SYMCRYPTO will be impacted by this vulnerability.

- An attacker with physical access to the device may be able to extract private keys through DPA given enough traces.

### Fix/Workaround

- No fix or mitigation is available.
