## Description

- Amnesia:33 is a batch of 33 vulnerabilities in several TCP/IP stacks reported here [https://www.forescout.com/research-labs/amnesia33/](https://www.forescout.com/research-labs/amnesia33/). The CVSS scores vary and are tabulated in the report. The highest score is 9.8.

- No Silicon Labs products are impacted since none of the TCP/IP stacks mentioned in report are used

### Fix/Workaround

- No fixes or work arounds for any of the issues in this report are required for any Silicon Labs products
