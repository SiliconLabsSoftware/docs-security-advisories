### Fix/Workaround

- A mitigation for [CVE-2019-19193](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2019-19193) is under active investigation. An updated advisory will be published when a mitigation becomes available.

- To mitigate the remaining vulnerabilities in this advisory, customers should update to Bluetooth Dual Mode SDK version 1.1.0 or later. The latest BT122 SDK can be found at [https://www.silabs.com/wireless/bluetooth/bluetooth-br-edr-modules/device.bt122](https://www.silabs.com/wireless/bluetooth/bluetooth-br-edr-modules/device.bt122).
