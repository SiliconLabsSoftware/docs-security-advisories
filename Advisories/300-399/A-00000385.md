### Source Product Impact Details

- Bluetooth Classic-capable products including BT121, BT122, RS9113 and RS9116

## Description

- Bluetooth Classic devices operating in connectable, non-discoverable mode can be tracked by a malicious third-party via a multi-step attack. The attack combines passive eavesdropping with active communication to determine the full Bluetooth MAC address to facilitate tracking.

- [CVE-2022-24695](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2022-24695) has been reserved for this vulnerability.

### Fix/Workaround

- This is a weakness in the Bluetooth protocol impacting all Bluetooth Classic, aka BR/EDR, devices which requires an update to the Bluetooth specification to define an interoperable mitigation. Silicon Labs will continue to monitor Bluetooth specification activity and comply with errata to the Bluetooth specification.
